Ruon Privacy Policy

Operator: KOREASIGN Co., Ltd. · Contact: ks42881@koreasign.co.kr · Tel +82-31-924-0477 App: Ruon (iOS com.koreasign.Ruon2, Android com.koreasign.Ruon2)

1. Summary

Ruon is a remote control for the Ruon hanji LED lamp. It works over your local Wi-Fi network. Ruon has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. KoreaSign does not receive your photos, videos, schedules or device settings.

2. What the app handles and where it goes

Data What happens Leaves your phone?
Photos and videos you pick Converted on your phone into the lamp's frame format, then sent over your local network only to your own Ruon lamp. Only to your lamp (local network). Never to KoreaSign.
Camera (QR scan) Used live to read the QR code printed on your Ruon. No image is saved or sent. No
Schedules, lamp names, brightness/volume, lamp connection details Stored in the app's private storage on your phone and sent to your lamp when you apply them. Only to your lamp
Local network / Wi-Fi The app finds, joins (Android) and talks to your lamp on your local network. Android asks for Location / Nearby devices permission because Android requires it to scan and join Wi-Fi networks; the app does not read or store your location. No

3. Permissions

  • Local network (iOS) / Wi-Fi, network state (Android): to discover and control your lamp.
  • Location / Nearby Wi-Fi devices (Android): required by Android to scan for and join the lamp's Wi-Fi. Not used to locate you.
  • Photos: to let you pick photos and videos to send to your lamp. (On Android the system photo picker is used and no storage permission is requested.)
  • Camera: to scan the QR code on your lamp.
  • Notifications (Android): to show the "Ruon connected" notification while the app keeps the connection to your lamp in the background (foreground service).

4. Retention and deletion

Everything the app stores is kept on your phone and on your lamp. Deleting the app removes the phone data; removing content or schedules from the lamp in the app removes it there. KoreaSign holds no copy, so there is nothing for us to delete on our side. You can reset your AI consent at any time in Menu → Reset AI data consent.

5. Children

Ruon is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

6. Changes

We will update this page and the date above if the app's data handling changes.

3. 권한

  • 로컬 네트워크(iOS) / 와이파이·네트워크 상태(Android): 조명을 찾고 조작하려고 써요.
  • 위치 / 주변 와이파이 기기(Android): Android가 조명 와이파이를 검색·접속할 때 요구해요. 위치 파악에는 쓰지 않아요.
  • 사진: 조명으로 보낼 사진·영상을 고르려고 써요. (Android는 시스템 사진 선택기를 써서 저장공간 권한을 묻지 않아요.)
  • 카메라: 조명의 QR 코드를 읽으려고 써요.
  • 알림(Android): 앱이 백그라운드에서 조명 연결을 유지하는 동안 "루온 연결됨" 알림을 보여 줘요(포그라운드 서비스).

4. 보관과 삭제

앱이 저장하는 정보는 모두 내 폰과 내 조명에만 있어요. 앱을 지우면 폰의 정보가 지워지고, 앱에서 조명의 콘텐츠·스케줄을 지우면 조명에서도 지워져요. 코리아사인은 사본을 갖고 있지 않아요. AI 동의는 메뉴 → AI 데이터 전송 동의 초기화에서 언제든 되돌릴 수 있어요.

5. 아동

루온은 만 13세(국내 만 14세) 미만 아동을 대상으로 하지 않으며, 아동의 개인정보를 알면서 수집하지 않아요.
